Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Regline 4.1

Translation of the French version, which prevails in case of difference.

In short
  • Regline needs no account and collects no personal data.
  • Your measurements, tables and files stay on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • No ads, no usage analytics, no trackers.
  • The only internet connection is the update check, which you can turn off.
  • "Enter on phone" uses only your local network, and only while you have it open.

Who is responsible

Regline is developed by Leandro Castro Moreira, in Switzerland. Questions about your data: contact@regline.ch.

The Regline app

Regline runs entirely on your computer. The data you type, paste or import (measurements, formulas, graphs, .regline files) is processed locally and saved only where you choose. Your settings (language, theme, preferences) are kept in a local file on your computer.

The My labs screen keeps, on your computer, the list of .regline files you have opened or saved and of the folders you chose to follow, to show them to you with a small preview. It does not search your whole computer and the list is sent nowhere. You can remove a file at any time (right-click → Remove from list).

If Regline crashes, it writes a short technical report to a file in your temporary folder (Regline-plantages.txt). This file stays on your computer: it is never sent automatically, but you may send it to me if you want to help fix the problem.

Regline contains no analytics, no automatic sending of crash reports and no advertising.

Buttons such as Support Regline, Privacy or the email address simply open your browser or mail app (for example regline.ch, where the donation links are). The app itself handles no payment and sends no data; what happens on the website is described below.

Enter on phone

The Enter on phone window lets you send measurements typed or scanned on your phone to your computer. Only while that window is open, Regline runs a small server on your local network (your Wi-Fi), at a random secret address shared through the QR code. Transfers are limited to 1 MB and the server stops as soon as you close the window.

Data stays between your phone and your computer: nothing goes through the internet or an external server. Nothing is kept except the text you choose to import into your table.

Update check

In the version downloaded from regline.ch, Regline checks at most once a day whether a new version exists. To do so, the app contacts the servers of GitHub (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft subsidiary, United States), where releases are published. As with any internet connection, GitHub then receives your IP address and basic technical information, including the installed Regline version number. Nothing else is sent, and Regline itself neither receives nor stores this information.

You can turn the check off at any time in Settings. If you accept an update, the installer is downloaded from GitHub and its SHA-256 checksum is verified before installation.

The Microsoft Store version does not check for updates: the Store handles them. It therefore never connects to the internet; it only uses your local network for "Enter on phone", and only when you open that window. The Microsoft Store applies its own privacy statement.

The regline.ch website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and operation (see GitHub's privacy statement). To show the latest available version, the home page also queries GitHub directly from your browser.

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no fonts or scripts loaded from other services. It exists in five languages: it reads the language set in your browser to suggest the right version, without sending anything, and remembers the language you choose, only in your browser. You can clear it by deleting the site's data.

Donations go through Stripe (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay) or Ko-fi (PayPal). These services process payment data themselves under their own privacy policies; I only receive the amount and, depending on the service, the donor's name or email address, which I use for nothing else. Links to GitHub also lead to an external service.

If you write to me

If you email contact@regline.ch, I use your address and message only to reply. They are never shared with third parties. Email is hosted by Infomaniak, in Switzerland.

Institutional licences

When an institution requests a quotation or buys an institutional licence, I use the details it provides (institution name, contact person, email, billing address) only for the quotation, invoice, licence and related support. They are never shared with third parties, except my bank for the payment. Invoices are kept for 10 years, as required by Swiss law.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, if you are in the European Union, the GDPR, you may ask which data concerns you and have it corrected or deleted. Since Regline collects nothing, there is usually nothing to request, but you can always write to contact@regline.ch.

Legal bases and retention

  • Update check and website hosting: legitimate interest in providing an up-to-date app and a safe, working website. Regline stores nothing; GitHub keeps its technical logs under its own policy.
  • Language chosen on the website: stored only in your browser; you can clear it at any time.
  • Emails: your message is used to reply to you (legitimate interest, or handling your request). I keep it for the conversation, then at most two years for bug follow-up, before deleting it.
  • Donations: Stripe and Ko-fi process payments under their own responsibility; I keep the donation information I receive as long as the law requires.

Complaints

If you think your data is not handled properly, please write to me first. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if you are in the European Union, the data protection authority of your country.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if a new feature connects to the internet), this page will be updated before the new version is released, with a new date at the top. It exists in five languages; in case of difference, the French version prevails.
