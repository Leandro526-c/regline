Regline 4.1 · Windows, Mac and Linux

Regline is built with high-quality open-source software used by scientists around the world. Thanks to their authors.

In short
  • This software keeps its own licences. Regline's licence agreement does not limit any of the rights they give you.
  • The full texts are in the app (Help → Licences and open-source software) and, on Windows, in the application's licences-tierces folder.
  • Regline itself is not open-source software: it is free for pupils, students and personal use (see the terms of use).

Qt and the LGPL: your rights

The Qt libraries are shipped as separate files in the application folder (for example the Qt6*.dll files on Windows). The GNU LGPL v3 lets you replace them with a compatible modified version of Qt, and reverse-engineer as needed to debug such modifications. Regline's licence agreement does not forbid this. To replace these files easily, use the installer downloaded from regline.ch rather than the Microsoft Store version, whose folder is protected by Windows.

The source code of Qt and Qt for Python is available at download.qt.io and code.qt.io. On request to contact@regline.ch, I will also send you a copy of the exact source code of the Qt versions used, at no cost other than shipping, for at least three years after the last distribution of the Regline version concerned.
